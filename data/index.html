How Ethanol Approximation Works

FuelGuard records approximately 100 consecutive conductivity readings over 10 seconds. The mean count and standard deviation are calculated on the ESP. Unstable or aerated samples are rejected.

Piecewise Monotonic Interpolation: With 2 or more distinct reference points (e.g. Sample A at E10 and Sample B at E20), FuelGuard calculates the estimated ethanol fraction of new fuel samples.

NOTE: Electrode depth, spacing, temperature, and electrolyte additives influence conductivity. Always keep electrodes clean and steady during testing.
